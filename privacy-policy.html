Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to AI Learning. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you create and use an AI School account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture
  • Age
  • Class information
  • Phone number
  • Student ID

Google Sign-In

Users may choose to sign in using Google. When you use Google Sign-In, information provided through your Google account may be used to create and manage your AI Learning account.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your student account
  • Personalize your learning experience
  • Display your profile information
  • Provide AI School services
  • Manage your subscription and account access
  • Improve the AI Learning service

Firebase Services

AI Learning may use Firebase services to help provide account authentication and store information needed for the operation of the application, including student account and profile information.

Payments

Payments for AI School subscriptions may be processed through Flutterwave. Payment information is processed through the payment provider according to its own systems and policies.

AI Learning does not claim to store sensitive payment card or bank details directly within the application.

Profile Information

Users may be able to update certain profile information, such as their full name and profile picture. Other information may be restricted from editing to help maintain account records.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect user information. However, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

Student Privacy

AI Learning is designed to support students and learning. We are committed to handling student information responsibly and will work to protect personal information used within the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any important changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact the AI Learning team through the official contact method provided by the application or website.
